A citizen ballot measure aiming to cap large donations in city races cleared signature thresholds and will appear on the autumn ballot, the elections office confirmed.

The measure limits contributions from a single source above a fixed threshold and requires faster online disclosure. Independent expenditures are harder to restrain and remain a known loophole.

The next hearing will tell whether the revised map is a genuine compromise or a device to run out the calendar. Housing advocates say they will measure acres and months, not adjectives.

Former council candidates are split. Some say they lost to late money; others say caps help incumbents who already have lists and free media.

A coalition of good-government groups will campaign for yes. A business PAC has not yet filed against, but lobbyists expect one.

Legal challenges are likely either way. Drafting attorneys say they wrote with prior court guidance in mind - and with humility about how courts evolve.
